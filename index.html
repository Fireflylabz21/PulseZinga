PulseZinga Compliance Disclaimers

Transparency, Editorial Guidelines & Usage Terms

1. News Aggregation Disclaimer

Editorial Transparency: PulseZinga is a news aggregator service. All curated stories, including featured headlines and breaking updates, are attributed to their respective publishers. We provide a neutral, structured interface for accessing trending news across multiple categories. Content freshness is maintained through automated ingestion, with articles typically displayed within their original publication window.

2. Financial Hub Disclaimer

Market Data Notice: Market indices, including Sensex and Nifty 50, and accompanying stock data (gainers/losers/commodity pricing) are fetched via third-party financial APIs (e.g., Yahoo Finance). Data is provided for informational purposes only. PulseZinga is not a financial advisor. Please verify all trading data, disclosures, and market movements directly through official exchange sources or your brokerage before making investment decisions.

3. AI Jobs & Careers Disclaimer

AI-Powered Career Portal: Our AI Jobs portal aggregates verified career opportunities from partner labs and open corporate feeds. We act as a directory, providing direct links to official company application portals. PulseZinga does not host or manage hiring processes and is not responsible for the accuracy of external job listings or recruitment outcomes.

4. AI & Data Usage Disclaimer

Artificial Intelligence (AI) Usage: PulseZinga utilizes AI technology to generate concise, structured summaries of news articles and market reports. While we implement rigorous automated guardrails to ensure factual grounding and maintain neutrality, users are encouraged to treat these summaries as quick insights and cross-reference them with primary, original sources for complete context.

5. Data Privacy & Policy

Privacy Commitment: PulseZinga is designed with privacy at its core.

  • Data Storage: User preferences and bookmarked content are stored locally on your device or securely handled via your Supabase instance.
  • No Data Exploitation: We do not sell, share, or monetize your personal usage data.
  • Transparency: By using this app, you consent to our automated data processing practices as detailed in our full Privacy Policy.
